# collapse meaning
To collapse means to fall down, cave in, or fail completely. The word can be used as both a verb (an action) and a noun (a state of breakdown). [1, 2, 3] 

Because it has a few different meanings depending on how it is used, they can be broken down by category:

## Physical Structures & Objects


* To cave in or break apart: A building, bridge, or roof falling down suddenly due to weight, damage, or strain (e.g., "The old roof collapsed under the heavy snow."). [1, 4, 5] 
* To fold neatly: An object designed to fold into a smaller, compact shape for easy storage (e.g., "This stroller collapses easily to fit into the car trunk."). [5, 6] 
  


## Health & Biology


* To faint or fall from exhaustion: A person or animal falling down or passing out due to illness, heat, or extreme fatigue (e.g., "The runner collapsed right after crossing the finish line."). [3, 4, 7] 
* Medical flattening: An organ or internal vessel losing its air or fluid and flattening out (e.g., a collapsed lung or a collapsed vein). [5, 8, 9] 
  


## Systems, Economies, & Abstract Concepts


* To fail completely: A sudden, total breakdown of an institution, organization, or agreement (e.g., "Talks between the two companies collapsed this morning.").
* Economic crash: A drastic, sudden loss of financial value or worth (e.g., "Fears are rising that the stock market might collapse."). [1, 2, 6, 8, 10]

# identical meaning
The word "identical" means exactly the same or alike in every single detail. [1, 2] 
## Key Meanings & Contexts


* The exact same entity (Numerical Identity): Referring to one single thing rather than two separate things (e.g., "This is the identical spot where we met last year"). [3, 4] 
* Exactly alike (Qualitative Identity): Referring to separate things that look or behave exactly the same (e.g., "They wore identical dresses"). [4, 5] 
* Biology: Used to describe twins (identical twins) who developed from a single fertilized egg and share the exact same genetic code. [6, 7] 
  

## Common Synonyms


* Same or Selfsame
* Indistinguishable
* Congruent (often used in geometry for matching shapes)
* Equivalent [3, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11]
