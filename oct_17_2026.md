shrink /SHriNGk/

A shrink is an informal or slang term for a mental health professional, such as a psychiatrist, psychologist, psychoanalyst, or psychotherapist.

Origin of the Term

* Headshrinker: The word comes from "headshrinker," a 20th-century slang term that humorously or derisively compared therapists to tribal headhunters (such as the Jivaroan peoples of the Amazon) who preserved shrunken heads.
* Popularization: The phrase gained traction in the United States during the 1950s through publications like Time magazine and Hollywood movies like Rebel Without a Cause. 
* Tone: While widely understood in US English, the term is casual, somewhat outdated, and can occasionally carry a negative or stereotypical connotation compared to proper titles like therapist or doctor.
Other Meanings of "Shrink"

* Verb: To become smaller or to make something smaller in size, volume, or value (e.g., a sweater shrinking in hot water). To draw back or recoil from fear or an unpleasant task.
* Retail / Accounting: The loss of inventory due to theft, damage, or administrative error (known as "retail shrinkage").
