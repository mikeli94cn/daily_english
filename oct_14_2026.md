To orchestrate means to arrange, organize, or coordinate a complex plan, event, or undertaking very carefully to achieve a desired result, or to compose/write music for an orchestra. 

Definitions and Usages

* Figurative / General Meaning (Most Common): To plan and direct a complicated situation, event, or strategy—often meticulously or behind the scenes—to make sure it turns out a specific way.
	* Example: "She orchestrated a brilliant publicity campaign for the movie's release."
* Musical Meaning (Literal): To write, score, or adapt a musical composition so that it can be performed by the individual instruments of an orchestra.
	* Example: "The composer spent months orchestrating the second act of the symphony."

Common Synonyms

* Organize, plan, coordinate, arrange, stage-manage, engineer, and mastermind.
